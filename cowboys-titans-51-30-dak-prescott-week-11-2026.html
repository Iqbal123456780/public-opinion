Cowboys Roll Past Titans 51-30 as Prescott Leads Offense

Dallas produced its highest-scoring game of the season, but the defensive problems and Tennessee’s long touchdowns remain a concern.

Published November 23, 2026 · By Public Opinion News Editorial Team

The Dallas Cowboys delivered a dominant offensive performance in a 51-30 win over the Tennessee Titans, scoring touchdowns on their first six drives. Dak Prescott threw four touchdown passes and added a rushing score, while CeeDee Lamb set a franchise milestone with another 1,000-yard season. The Cowboys Titans 51-30 result improved Dallas to 5-6, but the defense still allowed 30 points and several explosive plays. Tennessee fell to 1-9 and lost its fourth straight game. The victory gives Dallas momentum ahead of a short week and a Thanksgiving matchup with Philadelphia.

Dallas produced its highest-scoring game of the season

The Cowboys scored 51 points at AT&T Stadium, their first 50-point game since December 2022. Dallas reached the end zone on its first six possessions and scored on seven of eight drives before kneeling at the end. The offense controlled the pace and consistently created favorable situations.

The Cowboys Titans 51-30 scoreline reflected a large gap in finishing drives. The Cowboys Titans 51-30 result also showed how quickly Dallas can build a lead when the offense stays on schedule. Dallas converted early possessions into touchdowns, while Tennessee often settled for field goals. The Cowboys moved the ball through the air and on the ground, forcing the Titans to chase the game.

Dak Prescott delivered five total touchdowns

Prescott completed 28 of 33 passes for 325 yards and four touchdowns, with no interceptions, according to Reuters and NFL reporting. He also scored on a short rushing play. His accuracy helped Dallas sustain drives and avoid the turnovers that can erase scoring opportunities.

The quarterback’s performance was especially important after a difficult previous week. Dallas used tempo, spacing and matchup advantages to keep the defense reacting. Prescott’s ability to distribute the ball quickly gave several receivers opportunities and prevented Tennessee from concentrating its coverage on one target.

The opening drives set the tone

Dallas opened with a long drive that ended in a touchdown and continued to score on its next possessions. The offense mixed runs, short passes and deep throws, keeping the Titans from settling into a defensive rhythm. The early sequence gave the Cowboys a lead they never surrendered.

The Cowboys Titans 51-30 game featured six consecutive touchdown drives, a club milestone according to Associated Press reporting. Sustained drives also limited Tennessee’s opportunities to build momentum. The performance showed how the team can control a game when the line protects Prescott and the playmakers win their matchups.

CeeDee Lamb extended his 1,000-yard streak

Lamb caught 11 passes for 132 yards and a touchdown. He reached 1,000 receiving yards for the sixth consecutive season, setting a Cowboys franchise record and breaking the mark previously held by Michael Irvin. Lamb was a reliable option on short and intermediate routes.

His volume and efficiency helped Dallas stay ahead of the chains. The streak highlights consistency across seasons, not just one game. Lamb’s presence also draws coverage attention, opening opportunities for other receivers and the running game.

Javonte Williams and George Pickens added key scores

Javonte Williams scored twice, including a rushing touchdown and a receiving touchdown. George Pickens caught seven passes for 102 yards and a score. KaVontae Turpin and Brevyn Spann-Ford also reached the end zone, giving Dallas several scoring threats.

A diversified offense makes it harder for opponents to target a single player. Dallas spread the ball around and took advantage of Tennessee’s coverage gaps. The Cowboys’ running backs and receivers helped keep the offense balanced and supported Prescott’s passing numbers.

Tennessee found moments but could not keep pace

Cam Ward threw for 248 yards and two touchdowns and added a rushing score. Carnell Tate caught a 59-yard touchdown, while Tony Pollard rushed for 110 yards against his former team. Tennessee had productive plays but could not match Dallas’s scoring efficiency.

The Titans had to respond to a growing deficit, which increased pressure on their offense. Several drives ended in field goals, and a turnover late in the game helped Dallas add another touchdown. Tennessee’s performance showed some offensive potential, but it was not enough to offset the defense.

The Cowboys defense still allowed explosive plays

Dallas gave up 30 points and a long touchdown reception that kept Tennessee in the game for stretches. The defense allowed the Titans to move the ball and did not consistently prevent big gains. The Cowboys Titans 51-30 win therefore came with defensive questions. The Cowboys’ high-scoring offense masked some of those problems.

Quinnen Williams and James Houston recorded two sacks each, and Dallas forced a fumble that helped set up a late score. Those plays were valuable, but the defense will need better consistency against stronger opponents. A 51-point win does not automatically resolve its weaknesses.

The result improved Dallas’s record to 5-6

The Cowboys moved to 5-6, while Tennessee fell to 1-9 and extended its losing streak to four games. Dallas remains in the NFC East race, but it needs continued wins and help from other teams to improve its position. The standings make every remaining game important.

The Titans are rebuilding their season around a young quarterback and roster changes. Their next challenge is to reduce defensive mistakes and improve red-zone efficiency. Dallas has a different objective: prove that the offense can carry its form into games against tougher defenses.

Thanksgiving brings a quick test against Philadelphia

The Cowboys Titans 51-30 win gives Dallas momentum, but the team is scheduled to host Philadelphia on Thanksgiving, creating a short turnaround. The rivalry game will be a more demanding test of the Cowboys’ offense and defense. Philadelphia can pressure the quarterback and challenge Dallas’s ability to protect the ball.

The Cowboys will need recovery, preparation and a clear plan for the defensive issues exposed Sunday. A strong performance could build momentum in the division, while another high-scoring shootout may leave questions about the team’s ability to win consistently.

Conclusion

The Cowboys Titans 51-30 game showed Dallas at its offensive best. Prescott, Lamb and the supporting cast delivered a historic start, but Tennessee’s points and explosive plays exposed areas that still need work. Dallas has a short week before Philadelphia, making the win valuable but not a complete answer to its season-long challenges.
