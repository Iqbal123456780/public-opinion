Corum Powers Rams Past Packers on Thanksgiving Eve

The Rams leaned on a dominant ground attack and two late interceptions to beat Green Bay in the NFL's first Thanksgiving Eve game.

Published November 26, 2026 · By Public Opinion News Editorial Team

Blake Corum delivered the biggest rushing performance of his NFL career as the Los Angeles Rams beat the Green Bay Packers 26-13 on Wednesday night. Corum ran for 184 yards and a touchdown on 32 carries, helping the Rams control the second half at SoFi Stadium. The win extended Los Angeles' streak to five games and gave the NFL its first Thanksgiving Eve game. The Rams Packers Thanksgiving Eve game was decided by a powerful ground attack, a long second-half drive and defensive takeaways that stopped Green Bay from building a late response.

A 26-13 win gives Los Angeles a fifth straight victory

The Rams moved to 7-4 after beating Green Bay 26-13 on November 25. The Packers fell to 6-5 and suffered their second consecutive defeat. Reuters reported that Los Angeles outgained Green Bay 373 yards to 244 and held a 23-11 advantage in first downs. The Rams also controlled possession for 40 minutes and 40 seconds, compared with 19:20 for the visitors. Those numbers show how thoroughly the home side controlled the game after halftime. The result also kept the Rams moving in the right direction during a crowded NFC playoff race.

Blake Corum produced a career night on 32 carries

Corum carried the ball 32 times for 184 yards and one touchdown. The rushing total was a new career high and came on his 26th birthday. He repeatedly gained yards after contact and helped the Rams avoid difficult passing situations. The performance was also a response to the demands of a physical divisional-style matchup, even though Green Bay and Los Angeles play in different divisions. Corum's workload gave the offence a reliable rhythm and allowed the coaching staff to keep the ball away from the Packers for long stretches.

The Rams ground game created the decisive advantage

Los Angeles finished with 241 rushing yards on 47 carries. Green Bay managed only 55 yards on 14 attempts. Kyren Williams added 46 yards and a touchdown, while Corum supplied the explosive runs that repeatedly moved the chains. The Rams did not need a high-volume passing performance from Matthew Stafford because the run game controlled down and distance. A strong rushing attack also helped protect the defence by keeping the Packers offence on the sideline. That balance became more important as injuries reduced the Rams' available receiving options.

The game was level at halftime

The teams went into the break tied 13-13 after exchanging scoring drives. Williams scored on a seven-yard run to help Los Angeles take a 13-3 lead. Green Bay answered through a Jordan Love touchdown pass to Christian Watson and a 48-yard field goal from Trey Smack. The Packers had recovered from an early deficit and looked capable of turning the game into a close finish. The Rams needed a more sustained drive after halftime, and they found one immediately when the teams returned to the field.

An eight-minute drive changed the second half

Los Angeles opened the third quarter with a 64-yard drive that lasted eight minutes and two seconds. The 14-play possession ended with Corum scoring on a one-yard run. It gave the Rams a 20-13 lead and forced Green Bay to respond while facing a defence that was gaining confidence. The drive was important because it used time as well as yardage. The Packers had fewer opportunities to attack, and the Rams could continue to rely on Corum and Williams rather than asking Stafford to take unnecessary risks.

Stafford and Love had different kinds of nights

Stafford completed 16 of 23 passes for 140 yards and one interception. Love completed 17 of 29 passes for 198 yards, one touchdown and two interceptions. Green Bay's passing total was higher, but the Packers could not turn it into a sustained second-half comeback. The Rams were content to keep the offence balanced and avoid forcing the ball downfield. Love's two interceptions came at damaging moments, while Stafford's one turnover briefly offered Green Bay an opportunity that the defence could not convert into points.

The Rams defence created the key turnovers

Jaylen McCollough intercepted Love in the third quarter, and Emmanuel Forbes Jr. made another interception late in the fourth. The Rams also forced a fumble in the first half, recovered by Quentin Lake. Those plays helped compensate for the injuries affecting the home side's offence. Green Bay had chances to make the score closer, but its drives repeatedly ended without points. The defence's ability to create takeaways was as important as Corum's rushing total because it stopped the Packers from building momentum.

Christian Watson left with a concussion evaluation

Packers receiver Christian Watson caught four passes for 65 yards and a touchdown before leaving the game after a helmet-first hit late in the first half. The Rams' Kamren Kinchens was ejected for the hit, and Watson was evaluated for a concussion before being ruled out. The incident became one of the game's most serious moments. Injury assessments can change after the game, so the final status of a player should be taken from the team's latest medical update rather than assumed from the initial sideline report.

What the result means for both teams

The Rams' fifth straight win strengthened their position in the NFC race and showed that their offence can win through the ground game when the passing attack is quieter. Green Bay, now 6-5, needs to improve its run defence and protect the football after consecutive losses. The Packers remain in a competitive division, but the defeat increases the pressure on their next games. The Rams Packers Thanksgiving Eve game also offered a reminder that late-season form can change quickly when a team wins the line of scrimmage and limits its opponent's possessions.

What to know next: five quick questions

The result gives both teams a short period to review injuries, turnovers and the way they managed the clock. The Rams can build on their recent run, while Green Bay must respond quickly to avoid losing ground in the NFC North.
